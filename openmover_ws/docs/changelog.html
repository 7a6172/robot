11. Pedestrian collisions — proxy box removal

Gazebo Harmonic actors do not participate in physics collisions, so a pedestrian walking into the robot produced no contact and Nav2 had no reason to stop. Two solutions were explored in sequence:

ApproachStatusDetails
Proxy box removed First attempt: invisible collision boxes attached ahead of each actor so the LiDAR would "see" them. Abandoned — it is a simulation-only hack that boxes the LiDAR with geometry that does not exist in reality, and would mask the real perception problem.
Perception-based stop in use Final approach: the actor's feet are genuinely visible to the LiDAR; detections enter the local costmap like any obstacle, Nav2 stops in front of the pedestrian and resumes when the way is clear. Works exactly like it will on the real robot — no simulation-only machinery. Verified end-to-end; all proxy code/config deleted.

12. Package metadata & attribution

The stack aggregates work from several people: parts of the ROS 1 tree predate its listed maintainer, and the ROS 2 port is new work on top of it. Metadata now separates responsibility from authorship: <maintainer> records who currently maintains each ROS 2 package (the port author), while repeatable <author> entries preserve the original authors and credit the port explicitly. Per-file Doxygen headers in the toolboxes carry the same note.

The 20 toolbox source files with Doxygen author blocks each gained one line (ROS 2 Jazzy port by zar (zar@my.swjtu.edu.cn)) leaving the original author, affiliation and license untouched. Third-party assets inside the simulation package (URDF exporter boilerplate, mesh credits, model.config) were left as their authors published them.

Decision log (summary)

  • slam_toolbox over gmapping — maintained on Jazzy, better maps, no gmapping counterpart needed.
  • MPPI over DWB — Nav2's maintained controller; cost: controller_frequency 20.0 and retuned velocity limits.
  • manual bluesea2 port — no vendor Jazzy driver exists.
  • virtualizer dropped — output unused in the ROS 2 graph.
  • world rebuilt — Classic models are incompatible with Harmonic; layout, spawn pose and actor paths preserved.
  • perception-based pedestrian stop — replaces the proxy-box hack (see above).
  • attribution policy — maintainer = current port author; authors = original authors plus an explicit port note; licenses restored to the ROS 1 declarations (see above).
